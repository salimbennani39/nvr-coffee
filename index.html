Lost in Neima ▶

Specialty Coffee, Hand-poured V60 & Matcha

A ritual worth slowing down for.

Tucked behind a white lattice wall in Neima, Al Ain, NVR is a small specialty coffee spot built around one idea: the cup you drink every day should feel like a ritual, not a routine.

We hand-pour V60s in signature flavours, whisk matcha three ways, and pair them with cheesecake, brownies and cookies — served in the garden or straight to your door.

Some signatures include:

  • V60 Crème Brûlée(caramelised, silky, the guest favourite)
  • Matcha Salted Caramel(sweet, salty, rated 10/10 by guests)

Meet the mascot

The Turtlebehind every cup

Turtles guard our garden, ride on our lids and sit in our logo. They're a reminder of what we believe: good coffee shouldn't be rushed. Take the slow road, enjoy the pour, and make it yours.

Spot one on your cup? That's your sign to slow down.
